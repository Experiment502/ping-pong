## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent pixel-art PARK / SCHOOL BENCH sprite for a Pokemon Emerald-style top-down RPG.
Draw a beautiful, clearly readable wooden bench, replacing a crude horizontal tan bar in a game map. The bench faces SOUTH (toward the bottom of the screen) and is viewed from the CLASSIC GBA OVERHEAD perspective: we see the front of its upright BACKREST near the top, the TOP SURFACE of the seat in the middle, the front edge of the seat and two iron leg supports near the bottom. It must read as a real seat with depth, not a fence, table, or flat stripe.
Design: three warm honey-brown horizontal wood slats in the BACKREST separated by dark 1-pixel gaps, three slightly lighter seat planks, dark blue-gray cast iron end frames with two small armrests, two visible sturdy feet, subtle 1px dark stepped outline. Small highlights on upper wood edges, 3-4 flat shades per material, simple tiny woodgrain marks, a small tight translucent pixel shadow beneath its feet. Keep the seat open and usable.
Style: authentic low-resolution 16-bit handheld RPG tile art in Pokemon Emerald / Ruby Sapphire aesthetic, logical size about 72 pixels wide by 32 pixels high enlarged nearest-neighbor; crisp square pixels, hard edges, NO antialiasing, NO gradients, NO realistic 3D rendering, NO isometric diagonal angle. Long edges horizontal, symmetric bench silhouette. Palette: golden oak #bf925a, highlights #e2b87b, dark wood #725237, metal #475b60. Consistent lighting from upper left.
Composition: ONE centered bench only, on a genuinely transparent background, fully visible, fills most of wide landscape canvas. No floor, grass, scenery, characters, text, labels, border, atlas, arrows, logos. Entire asset must fit roughly a 2.25:1 silhouette. This will be placed in a Japanese elementary school courtyard, corridors, and community park alongside 52px tall chibi characters; make it cohesive and detailed enough to remain readable at 96x42 pixels.
