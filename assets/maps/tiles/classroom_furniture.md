# Classroom furniture

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using indoor.png as the style and palette reference. Assets: teacher_lectern.png and student_desk.png. Both retain the generated transparency.

The lectern is 2x1 map tiles, with a closed front facing the students and no attached chair. The teacher stands behind it. Each north-facing student desk/chair set occupies 1x2 tiles; the aspect-preserving renderer displays it about 32x45 pixels, beside characters rendered in a 36x52 frame. Its chair back faces the camera and the open sitting side faces the desk/blackboard. Aisles remain between columns and rows.

## Teacher lectern prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent furniture sprite for an existing GBA-era top-down pixel RPG. Input reference: indoor.png is a STYLE / PALETTE reference ONLY, use its warm oak wood, dark outlines, 3 flat shading tones and fixed overhead game camera. Primary request: a Japanese/Taiwan elementary classroom TEACHER LECTERN / teaching desk, seen from above and south. Wide horizontal oak top, substantial simple closed wooden front modesty panel facing the students at BOTTOM of image, two short feet. Teacher stands behind it toward TOP and faces SOUTH. No chair or seated person. One tiny closed attendance book on one corner; mostly clear tabletop. Physical scale: roughly twice a single-student desk width; final sprite about 60 pixels wide by 32 pixels tall, aligned with screen axes. Draw as low-resolution 16-bit pixel sprite enlarged nearest-neighbor; crisp pixel clusters and edges, no gradients, no isometric diagonal. Center one complete isolated object on a genuinely transparent canvas with generous padding. No floor, backdrop, classroom scene, lettering, labels, grids, shadows outside footprint, border, watermark, other furniture. Match the existing game's approved wood furniture rather than changing style.

Desk at TOP/far side, chair at BOTTOM/near side; show the OUTSIDE REAR of chair backrest, with narrow glimpse of green seat ahead of it. A seated student's gaze would point UP. Small plain warm-oak rectangular tabletop, thin dark gray steel legs, a single small chair with muted green backrest and seat, narrow steel frame. NOT a home study desk, NO built-in drawers or large pedestal, NO open book on every table, no double desk. Desk width should be about 1.5 times a child's shoulder width. Top-down GBA 16-bit pixel art, matching indoor.png warm wood and outlines, 3 flat tones per material, crisp low resolution pixels enlarged nearest-neighbor. Long axes exactly screen-horizontal and screen-vertical, no diagonal isometric. One entire joined desk/chair sprite centered on genuinely transparent canvas with generous padding.
