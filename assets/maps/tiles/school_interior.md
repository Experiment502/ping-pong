## Architecture reference

Benches are present in some actual Japanese school corridors: [Tachikawa Eighth Elementary School renovation](https://www.city.tachikawa.lg.jp/kosodate/gakko/1004249/1004338.html). Their removal here is a layout choice for the game's classroom corridors.
